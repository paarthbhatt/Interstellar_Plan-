# Cost Analysis: The Price of Survival

You asked: **"How much will it cost?"**

While "Money is no object" was the premise, putting a price tag on the project demonstrates its feasibility. Unlike the creation of a Warp Drive (Physics Impossible), this project is merely **Expensive**.

## Total Estimated Program Cost: **$3.5 Trillion USD**
*(Spread over 20 years: $175 Billion / Year)*

---

## 1. The Infrastructure (The Laser Array) - **$3 Trillion**
The dominant cost is not the ship, but the "Road" (The Laser Array).
*   **Item:** 100 GW Orbital Phased Array.
*   **Hardware Cost:** The lasers.
    *   *Current Cost:* High-power fiber lasers cost ~$10/Watt.
    *   *Mass Production Required:* 100 Gigawatts ($100 \times 10^9$ Watts).
    *   *Scale Economy:* We estimate dropping costs to $20/Watt (Space-rated).
    *   *Total:* **$2 Trillion**.
*   **Launch Cost:**
    *   *Mass:* Assuming 1kg/kW density = 100,000 Metric Tons.
    *   *launch Vehicle:* SpaceX Starship ($10M per 100 tons).
    *   *Flights:* 1,000 flights.
    *   *Total:* **$10 Billion** (Surprisingly cheap).
*   **Assembly & Power:** Nuclear reactors to power the array. **$1 Trillion**.

## 2. The Vessel ("Corrugated Clipper") - **$250 Billion**
Building the actual ship is comparable to the Space Station program.
*   **R&D (Nanolaminates):** $50 Billion.
*   **Hull & Life Support:** $100 Billion (Complex closed-loop systems).
*   **The Sail:** $10 Billion (Material cost is low, fabrication is high).
*   **Gravity System:** $5 Billion.

## 3. Operating Costs (The "Gas") - **$50 Billion**
*   **Energy:** Running a 100 GW laser for 1 year (Acceleration Phase).
*   **Consumption:** 876 Terawatt-hours (TWh).
*   **Cost:** At space-nuclear rates ($0.05/kWh) = **$43.8 Billion**.

---

## Comparison Context
*   **US Defense Budget (1 Year):** ~$850 Billion.
*   **US GDP (1 Year):** ~$28 Trillion.
*   **Global Energy Infrastructure Value:** >$100 Trillion.

**Verdict:**
This project costs roughly **4 years of the US Defense Budget**.
For a planetary civilization facing an existential threat, this is **highly affordable**.
