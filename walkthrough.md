# Project Longshot: The Road to 1% Light Speed

We have successfully engineered a practical solution to reach **3,000 km/s** (1% $c$) using existing physics and technology.

## 1. The Final Architecture: "The Corrugated Clipper"
We abandoned the brute-force Nuclear Pulse (Orion) method in favor of a scalable **Laser-Driven Light Sail** approach, optimized with 2025-era materials research.

### **The Engine: Phased Laser Array**
*   **Infrastructure:** A 100 GW orbital laser array.
*   **Physics:** Momentum transfer from photons. No onboard fuel required.

### **The Sail: Corrugated Nanolaminate** (Campbell et al. 2025)
*   **Material:** Alumina/Molybdenum Disulfide ($<1 \text{ g/m}^2$).
*   **Structure:** Hexagonal corrugation for rigidity.
*   **Stability:** Photonic Metasurface (Lin et al. 2025).

### **The Gravity: "Gemini" Tether System**
*   **Habitation:** Twin 50-ton modules connected by a **450m Zylon tether**.
*   **Method:** Spinning at **2 RPM** to generate **1g**.
