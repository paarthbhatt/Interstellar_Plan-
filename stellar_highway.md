## 1. The Physics of Scaling
The beauty of this approach is in the **Power Density**.
*   **Formula:** Thrust $F = \frac{2P}{c}$ (assuming perfect reflection).
*   **Scaling:** To push heavier things, you don't necessarily need *more* power; you can use the *same* power over a *longer* time and larger area, provided you manage the **Diffraction Limit** (keeping the beam focused).

### The "4% Absorption" Breakthrough
You mentioned nanolaminates with **<1 g/m²** density but **4% absorption**.
*   **For Gram-scale probes:** 4% absorption is fatal (vaporization) because the laser focus is tight (GW/m²).
*   **For Heavy Ships:** 4% absorption is **perfectly acceptable**.
    *   *Calculation:* Spreading 100 GW over a 1 km² sail (for a heavy ship) gives an intensity of 100 kW/m² (approx 100x Suns).
    *   Absorbing 4% (4 kW/m²) results in an equilibrium temperature of **~160°C (433 K)**.
    *   **Conclusion:** This validates your material choice. We can use these robust, corrugated sails for the heavy missions.

## 2. The Roadmap (Baby Steps)

### Phase I: "The Spark" (Ground-Based Testbed)
*   **Goal:** 1% Light Speed for **10g Payloads** (Nano-Sensors).
*   **Infrastructure:** 1 MW Ground Laser Array (10m diameter).
*   **Vehicle:** "WaferSat" (10g chip on a 10m sail).
*   **Result:** Proves beam pointing and stability.
*   **Status:** Achievable immediately.

### Phase II: "Starshot" (The Scientific Probe)
*   **Goal:** 20% Light Speed for **1g Payloads** OR 1% Light Speed for **1 kg Payloads**.
*   **Infrastructure:** 100 GW Ground Laser Array (1 km diameter).
*   **Vehicle:**
    *   *Option A (Speed):* 1g StarChip. Reaches 0.2c in minutes. Needs ultra-reflective sails (99.9999%).
    *   *Option B (Utility):* 1 kg "CubeSat". Reaches 0.01c (3,000 km/s) in ~1.5 hours.
*   **The Problem:** Atmosphere distorts the beam, and the range is limited to ~0.4 AU before the beam spreads too wide.
*   **Verdict:** Excellent for fast flybys, but hard to scale to heavy ships due to atmospheric scattering.

### Phase III: "The Pathfinder" (Heavy Robotic Probe)
*   **Goal:** 1% Light Speed for **1 Ton (1,000 kg)** payloads.
*   **Infrastructure:** **Orbital Phased Array (10 km diameter)**.
    *   *Upgrade:* We move the 100 GW array to High Earth Orbit (HEO) or Lagrange Point L2.
    *   *Why:* In space, we can maintain beam coherence over **40-50 AU** (distance to Pluto).
*   **Vehicle:** 1-Ton Probe with a **1 km² Corrugated Nanolaminate Sail**.
    *   Sail Mass: 1,000 kg (at 1 g/m²).
    *   Payload: 1,000 kg.
*   **Performance:**
    *   Acceleration: ~0.33 m/s².
    *   Time to 1% c: ~100 Days.
    *   Distance covered: ~150 AU.
*   **Thermal Load:** Only ~160°C (using your MoS2/Alumina material).
*   **Significance:** This allows us to send heavy equipment to the Oort cloud or neighboring stars.

### Phase IV: "The Stellar Highway" (Human Scale)
*   **Goal:** 1% Light Speed for **100 Ton** (Crewed) Vessel.
*   **Infrastructure:** **100 TW** Space-based Array OR **Massive Lens**.
    *   Scaling up the Phase III array to 1000 km diameter (virtual aperture using swarm drones).
*   **Vehicle:** "Light Clipper".
    *   Mass: 100 Tons.
    *   Sail: 100 km diameter (Ultra-thin mesh).
*   **Performance:**
    *   Pushes the vessel to 3,000 km/s over a period of **1-2 years**.
*   **Survival:** The "slow" acceleration (milli-g) is actually better for the crew and structure.
